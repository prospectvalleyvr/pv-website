Help & FAQ

How may I help?

Answers to the questions travellers ask most.

H

Herald, your concierge

Lost? Splendid – that's how most adventures begin. Ask away; if the answer isn't below, write to us and a real person will reply.

What is Prospect Valley?

A connected set of VRChat worlds: a game, a Realm of land you can call home, and the PV Blue Pages directory. It begins in the Celestial Garden.

How do I start?

Visit the Celestial Garden in VRChat. Walk the path of the three tests, choose your calling and origin, answer the heart question, and step through the Birth Gate.

Herald: Take your time. The Garden has been waiting a while; another minute won't hurt it.

What is my Soul Scroll?

A code that carries your character – calling, origin, strengths, places you've visited – from one Prospect Valley world to the next. Copy it at any Scroll Desk and read it in the next world.

How do I get a free Homestead?

Make your claim code at the Scroll Desk beside the Birth Gate. When the Land Rush opens, enter it on this website with your VRChat display name. One free Homestead per player, renewed once a year for free.

Is a Homestead an investment?

No. A Homestead is a free licence to use your plot in the game. It does not earn money and is not sold for profit.

What are PV Bucks and PV Chips?

PV Bucks are the Realm's currency for shops and markets. PV Chips are for games only – they are never sold and never swapped for anything. Nothing in the game can be cashed out for real money.

My claim code ran out.

Codes last 30 days. Visit the Scroll Desk again and make a fresh one – it's free.

Who can play?

Prospect Valley's own worlds are for everyone 13 and over, following VRChat's own age rules.

Something isn't working.

Write to Info@ProspectValleyVR.com with the world's name and what happened.

Herald: The owner records are sometimes still in the post. Even I can't hurry the post.
