Privacy

Privacy policy

Draft version 0.1 · 31 October 2026 · being reviewed by our attorney and may change before Homestead claims open.

This is a working draft written in plain English. If anything here is unclear, write to Info@ProspectValleyVR.com and we'll answer you.

Who we are

Prospect Valley is made by PVVR, LLC ("Prospect Valley VR", "we"). This policy covers this website and the Prospect Valley account you can make here. Inside VRChat, VRChat's own privacy policy also applies.

Browsing

Reading this website collects nothing about you: no advertising, no tracking cookies, no analytics. The Land Rush countdown reads a public file and sends nothing about you. Our hosting provider (Cloudflare) handles your connection, including your IP address, to deliver the pages and protect the site from attacks; we don't use it to identify you.

What we keep if you make an account

  • Sign-in: the account number your sign-in service (Discord, later Google) gives us, and the date you joined. Never your password.
  • Email: the email address your sign-in service shares, if it is verified. Used only for Homestead renewal reminders, important notices about your account, and replies to you. No marketing unless you ask for it.
  • VRChat display name you type, whether a claim code has verified it, and whether you ticked "I am 18 or older".
  • From your claim code: your origin, calling and heart answer, and the date the code was made. Used codes are kept as a one-way fingerprint so each code works once.
  • Your Homestead: the plot, when you claimed it, its renewal date and whether you have used your free move.
  • Safety records: how many codes you tried in the last hour (to stop guessing), and a record of actions our admins take.
  • One sign-in cookie that keeps you signed in for up to 30 days, and a short-lived cookie (10 minutes) that protects the sign-in step. We store sign-ins only in a scrambled (hashed) form.

What becomes public

So the worlds can show who lives where, we publish a public list of claimed plots. For each plot it shows the plot number and type – and nothing that names you.

  • 18 or older, with a verified name: your plot also carries a short code worked out from your VRChat display name (an "owner code"). It lets you use Find my home in the worlds. Anyone who knows your exact display name could work out the same code and find your plot, just as they could find you in VRChat. Your name itself is never published unless a future setting lets you choose to show it.
  • Under 18, or not ticked 18+: your plot is published with no owner code at all and you can't be found by others. Your Homestead is shown to you on this website instead.

The public list is kept in a public GitHub repository. When a plot changes owner or is released, the current list is updated within about 15 minutes, but earlier versions of the list remain in that repository's history.

Who we share with

We never sell or rent your information, and we don't share it for advertising. We use these services to run the site, and they handle data for us under their own terms: Cloudflare (hosting and our database), Discord and later Google (only to sign you in), and GitHub (hosting the public plot list described above). We may disclose information if the law requires it or to protect players and the service.

Age

Prospect Valley is for players 13 and older, matching VRChat. If you are under 13, please don't make an account; if we learn one belongs to a child under 13 we will delete it. Players under 18 can hold a free Homestead, but are never shown by name and can't be found by others.

How long we keep it

We keep your account until you delete it. Fingerprints of used claim codes are kept so a code can't be used again. Expired sign-ins are cleared regularly. Our hosting provider's backups may hold deleted data for up to about 30 days.

Your choices and rights

  • See and download your data from your account page at any time.
  • Correct your display name and 18+ tick on your account page.
  • Delete your account on your account page. It happens straight away; your Homestead returns to the Realm and leaves the public list at the next update.
  • Wherever you live, you can write to us to ask about, correct or delete your information, or to complain. You may also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority.

Security

We keep the minimum, store sign-ins and used codes only in hashed form, keep secret keys out of our code, and limit admin access to the owners. No system is perfect; if something goes wrong that affects you, we will tell you.

Changes

We'll post any change here with a new version date, and email account holders about important changes.
