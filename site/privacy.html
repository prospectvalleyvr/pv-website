Privacy

Privacy

A short, plain notice while the website is being built. A full privacy policy will be published before Homestead claims open.

What we keep

Browsing this website collects nothing about you: no advertising, no tracking cookies, no analytics. The Land Rush page reads a public file to show the countdown and sends nothing about you.

If you create an account (sign in with Discord, or Google when available), we keep only:

  • the account number your sign-in provider gives us, so we recognise you next time – never your password;
  • your email address, if your provider shares a verified one – used only for Homestead renewal reminders and replies to you;
  • the VRChat display name you type, and whether you ticked "I am 18 or older";
  • from a claim code you check: your origin, calling and heart answer, and the date the code was made;
  • one sign-in cookie, which keeps you signed in for up to 30 days.

We never sell or share this information. Players under 18 – or anyone who hasn't ticked the 18+ box – are never shown by name and can't be found by others on the public owner lists.

Your choices

On your account page you can download your data or delete your account at any time. Deleting removes it straight away.
