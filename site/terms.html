2. Who can join

You must be 13 or older (and old enough to use VRChat where you live). If you are under 18, please make sure a parent or guardian is happy for you to join. Tick "I am 18 or older" only if it's true.

3. Your account

  • One account per person. Link only your own VRChat display name; a claim code made in the world proves it.

4. Your free Homestead

  • What it is: a free, personal, limited licence to use one plot in the Realm of Prospect Valley, for as long as you keep it renewed. It is not ownership of property, land or any share in Prospect Valley, and it has no cash value.
  • One per player. Using extra accounts, other people's names or automated tools to get more than one Homestead isn't allowed, and extra Homesteads will be released.
  • How plots are given out: in one fixed, public order, nearest the Wonder first. You see each offered plot's resources and features before you choose, and the first player to complete a claim on a plot gets it. There is no draw, no chance and no payment.
  • Renewal: once a year, free, with a fresh code from the worlds. If a Homestead isn't renewed, it returns to the Realm after a grace month, and we'll email you reminders first if we have your email.
  • Free move: while only Giza is open, your Homestead is placed in Giza; you can move it once, free, to your heart's own Land when that Land opens.
  • Changes to the Realm: it's a game in development. We may need to move, reshape or reset plots, features or resources for fairness, safety, performance or the game's story; we'll give notice where we can and treat players fairly.
  • Leaving: if you delete your account, your Homestead returns to the Realm and becomes free for another player.

Homesteads, plots, resources, PV Chips and anything else in Prospect Valley are for play only. They are not an investment, we make no promise that they will gain value, and they can't be exchanged for real money with us. Selling, renting or trading accounts or Homesteads for real money or anything outside the game isn't allowed. PV Chips are play-only and are never real-money gambling. Paid land and a player market are not offered; if they ever are, they will come with their own terms.
