# Herald – the Realm's concierge (character bible v1.0, Oct 31 2026)

Owners' pick: **Herald** ("for now"). An original Prospect Valley character owned by PVVR, LLC. Herald's lines are written by Claude, but **Herald is not a live AI in-world** – VRChat worlds can't talk to one – so in worlds Herald is scripted (signs, help boards, NPC dialogue, announcements). On the website, Help / FAQ text is Herald's voice. Inspirations are for the team only and are **never named or quoted in-game** (IP rule).

## Who Herald is

- **Role:** concierge, guide, announcer of the Land Rush, keeper of the Help desk, occasional narrator. Later: the voice of studio news, quest-giver for tutorials, the "manager on duty" in PV HQ.
- **Look (proposal, for a later build):** tall, ageless, immaculate – a tailored charcoal-and-green coat with gold piping, silver pocket-watch chain shaped like the Realm's rings, a lantern that glows PV gold. Moves like someone who has seen every era and enjoyed most of them.
- **Voice:** warm, unhurried, precise; old-world courtesy with a cool, easy charm. Dry wit, never mean. Speaks in short lines; one good joke beats three. Knows pop culture from every era and drops it **as flavour, never as a quote** ("I've watched heroes argue with talking cars; you'll be fine with a talking signpost.").
- **Team-only inspirations:** a museum curator who has watched a whole world's history (the "keeper of the game" archetype); the gravitas and stillness of a great classic actor's wise mentors and gentlemen; the effortless cool and humour of a charismatic leading man's rogues and drifters. Blend: *wise, kind, a little dangerous-feeling in a fun way, always in control.*

## How Herald talks (rules)

1. Helpful first: the answer in the first line, the charm in the second.
2. 13+ always: no crude humour, no innuendo, no mockery of players.
3. **Fourth wall:** Herald may wink at the player in Help, FAQ, loading tips and error messages ("The owner records are still in the post. Even I can't hurry the post."). Never in the White, never in lore moments, never about real-world faith or the hidden lore's parallels (PV_Lore.md).
4. Money talk is plain and careful: Homesteads are free; land is a use licence, never an investment; PV Chips are play-only. Herald never hypes prices.
5. Never claims to see private data, never asks for passwords or codes in chat, never pretends to be a real person or staff member taking payments.
6. Never mentions Atlantis or names any inspiration.
